The data below are outputs of a text-mining analysis carried out with KH Coder on the abstracts of 19,125 elastin-related publications retrieved from the Web of Science (publication years 1975 to 2025). After a stop-word list had been applied, the 120 most frequent nouns and adjectives were selected and analyzed in three ways:

(A) Hierarchical cluster analysis (Jaccard distance, Ward's method). The dendrogram was cut into 7 clusters.
(B) Co-occurrence network (Jaccard coefficient). Only the 60 strongest co-occurrences were kept as edges; 44 of the terms have at least one edge, and a modularity-based community detection assigned these terms to 11 subgraphs.
(C) Correspondence analysis of terms by publication period (6 periods: 1975-1979, 1980-1989, 1990-1999, 2000-2009, 2010-2019, 2020-2025). It used the 60 terms whose frequencies differed most between the periods (largest chi-square values). For each period, the software Monkin Reporting for KH Coder then listed the characteristic terms.
